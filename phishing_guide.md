# phishing awareness guide

## what is phishing? phishing is a cyberattack that uses deceptive messages to trick people into giving away their private information

## red flags to watch out on
**urgency:**if the message threatens one to take action under pressure scammers just wants them to not think straight
**unusual sender address:**their sender address is unfamiliar with lots of typos and weird do main so its safe that one checks that first
**generic greetings:**if its from a real company the greetings would contain the users name than just being broad
**shady links:**their emails URL are usually messed up hence its safe to not click the link rather run it through the officials to check its authenticity.

##quick rules to stay safe
**double-check the source:**run it through official legit sites rather than just rushing to click on the links 
**turn on MFA:**always enable multifactor authentication to reduce the high risks of scammers trying to get into your accouts because the password on its own is not enough
**dont rush:**the best defence is to take your time to look through weird emails before clicking on them.
